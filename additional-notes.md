# Additional Notes

## Additional Findings and Questions

The incident demonstrates the significant operational risks ransomware poses to healthcare organizations that rely heavily on digital medical systems and patient records.

Additional investigation should determine:
- whether backups were available and unaffected,
- how many systems were compromised,
- whether sensitive patient information was exfiltrated,
- and whether phishing awareness training had previously been conducted for employees.

The organization should review email security controls, implement stronger phishing detection mechanisms, and improve incident response planning to reduce the likelihood of future ransomware incidents.
