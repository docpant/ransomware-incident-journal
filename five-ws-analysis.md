# The 5 W's Analysis

## Who caused the incident?

An organized group of unethical hackers targeting healthcare and transportation organizations carried out the ransomware attack using phishing emails and malicious attachments.

---

## What happened?

Employees received phishing emails containing malicious attachments. Once downloaded, malware was installed on company systems, allowing attackers to deploy ransomware that encrypted critical organizational files and disrupted healthcare operations.

---

## When did the incident occur?

The security incident occurred on a Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 a.m. Employees began reporting inaccessible systems and encrypted files shortly after arriving at work.

---

## Where did the incident happen?

The ransomware attack affected the internal network and computer systems of a small U.S. healthcare clinic specializing in primary-care services.

---

## Why did the incident happen?

The incident occurred because employees interacted with phishing emails containing malicious attachments. The attackers exploited human error and insufficient phishing defenses to gain unauthorized access to the organization’s network and deploy ransomware.
