# Incident Timeline

## Timeline of Events

### 9:00 a.m. — Initial Reports

Employees reported being unable to access medical records and critical business applications required for healthcare operations.

---

### 9:05 a.m. — Ransom Note Identified

Multiple systems displayed a ransom note claiming organizational files had been encrypted by attackers demanding payment for a decryption key.

---

### 9:15 a.m. — Phishing Email Investigation

Initial investigation identified phishing emails containing malicious attachments that had been sent to several employees before the ransomware deployment.

---

### 9:30 a.m. — Malware Infection Confirmed

Security personnel confirmed that opening the malicious attachment installed malware that allowed attackers to gain unauthorized access to organizational systems.

---

### 10:00 a.m. — Ransomware Deployment Analysis

The ransomware encrypted critical healthcare files, preventing employees from accessing patient records and operational systems.

---

### 10:30 a.m. — Business Operations Disrupted

Healthcare operations were severely impacted as employees lost access to essential applications and patient information systems.

---

### 11:00 a.m. — Incident Escalation

The organization shut down affected systems and contacted external organizations and technical specialists for incident response assistance.
